Mole Map every route goes through moles · against an arrow = do the opposite

Setting it up
  1. Write the given: number + unit + what it's of.
  2. Trace a path on the map to what you want.
  3. Each arrow = one fraction. Unit you have goes on the bottom so it cancels.
Numbers
  • MM = Σ (sub × AM), each AM to 2 decimals
  • (NO3)2 → N 2, O 6 · hydrate ·5H2O → add 5 × 18.02
  • Metal or NH4 in it → formula units, else molecules
  • Sig figs = the given's (sub, MM, 6.02×1023 are exact)
Ex 36.0 g H2O → all atoms: 36.0 g × 1 mol18.02 g × 6.02×1023 molec1 mol × 3 atoms1 molec = 3.61×1024 atoms
